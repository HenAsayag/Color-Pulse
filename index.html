How to play

The thin white marker at twelve o’clock never moves. The ring of colours turns underneath it. Press once — tap the playfield, click, or use Space — at the moment a colour is under the marker.

  • Yellow +1
  • Blue +2
  • Green +5 narrow, hardest
  • Orange +3 and restores a heart

Every press reverses the ring. Each time you strike, the colours start turning the other way — so the next target comes at you from the opposite side.

Pressing over a dark gap costs a heart. So does letting the inner ring run all the way down — you have 6 seconds to land each strike. Three hearts, and the run ends when the last one goes.

Scoring values, the timer, spawn rates and the menus are reconstruction defaults chosen for this build, not rules recovered from the original game.
